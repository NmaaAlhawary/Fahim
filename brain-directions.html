Bone-white matte polycarbonate, 18 mm corners, 11 mm thick with a flat back. Four magnets in the back so it snaps onto a steel plate in any chassis. One continuous light ring instead of ten LEDs.
Best for
Global online buyers and design-minded parents. Ages 10 and up. Photographs well, ages well, and the accent colour can change per tier without new tooling.
Tradeoff
Thin means a thinner cell, roughly 1200 mAh, and no room for a speaker larger than 20 mm. Least warm of all six for a six-year-old.

Glossy ABS with grip bulges at both ends, screen recessed in a grape bezel. Adds a four-way D-pad on the left. The ten LEDs become one colour bar under the screen.

Ages 8 to 13, the kid who would rather be gaming. Remote-controlling the robot needs no phone, and lessons can be framed as levels.

Shell
High-gloss white polycarbonate, pill-shaped, no visible screws. One black glass window covers the screen; a clear light-guide ring around it turns the ten LEDs into a smooth halo that can shift colour with mood.
Best for
All three tiers and every market. Reads as a consumer robot, not a school board, so it sells on a shelf and in a photo. The natural head for the pet robot.
Tradeoff
Gloss shows fingerprints and scratches; white resin needs UV stabiliser or it yellows. The light-guide ring is one more clear molded part, and the black window hides the clip pads' neighbourhood, so the electronics feel is lowest of all seven.
