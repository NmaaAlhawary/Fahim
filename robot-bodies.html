The battery is the brain
The brain's cell feeds the motor rail, so the body carries motors, servos, sensors and a small cradle board, and no battery of its own. Dashed parts sit inside the shell.

Why a pet, not a rover
Every onboard sensor becomes a behaviour a child can name: hear, feel, see, react. The four servos use all four servo headers, and the spare clip pad becomes the touch pad. And the brain slides out of the head pod in one move, so the same head can go into the next kit.

Why a companion, not a rover
A companion turns every onboard sensor into a relationship: it hears, feels touch, sees you coming, and answers with its body. It never drives off a table. Every port on the brain is used, and the head still lifts off in one move for the electronics lessons. One caveat: a face this size wants a bigger screen than 1.54″. The visor glass widens the look for now, but this is the strongest argument for the 1.9″ option in the spec's open questions.
